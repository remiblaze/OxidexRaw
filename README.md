# Oxidex Raw: one-knob analog tape character

![Oxidex Raw free one-knob analog tape character UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/OxidexRaw/main/oxidexraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up for instant analog tape, warmth, saturation, and gentle wow/flutter that auto-levels as you push it.**

Oxidex Raw is the free, one-knob version of Oxidex. A single **AGE** macro takes you from a pristine studio reel to a worn, warm tape sound, no menus, no tweaking.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/OxidexRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`OxidexRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Oxidex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`OxidexRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Oxidex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
- **AGE**: the one knob. Turn it up and the tape ages: warmth builds into heavier saturation, the low end gets a growing head-bump, the highs roll off gently, and a touch of tape hiss eases in. Output is auto-leveled so you hear character, not just more volume.

## 💻 System Requirements

**macOS**
- macOS 15.0 or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel Mac (Universal Binary)
- An AU, VST3, CLAP or AAX host

**Windows**
- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- A VST3 or CLAP host
